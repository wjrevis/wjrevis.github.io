# Shorts Autopilot

Shorts Autopilot is a private tool built by Will Revis to produce and post videos for the First Layer News YouTube channel, which covers 3D printing news, tips and deals.

It is used only by its owner and only accesses the owner's own YouTube channel, Google Sheet and Google Drive folders.
