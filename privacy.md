# Privacy Policy for Shorts Autopilot

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Shorts Autopilot is a private tool operated by Will Revis to create and publish videos for the First Layer News YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and is used only by its owner.

## What data it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, Shorts Autopilot accesses the owner's own YouTube channel (to upload videos and read channel analytics), the owner's own Google Sheet (to store scripts, a posting calendar and performance reports) and the owner's own Google Drive folders (to read voice recordings and video clips). It does not access data belonging to anyone else.

## How data is used

This data is used only to produce, schedule and upload videos and to measure how they perform. Script text and recording transcripts may be processed by AI services to draft and edit videos.

## Sharing

Shorts Autopilot does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. Data is not used for advertising.

## Storage and deletion

Data stays in the owner's own Google account and private code repository. Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Google API Services

Shorts Autopilot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
